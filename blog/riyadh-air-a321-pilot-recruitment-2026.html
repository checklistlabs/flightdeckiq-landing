Riyadh Air Opens A321neo Pilot Recruitment: What Captains and FOs Need to Know

September 2026 4 min read FlightDeckIQ

In this article

  1. What's New: The A321neo Track
  2. What's Confirmed
  3. What's Being Reported (Unconfirmed)
  4. How to Prepare

Riyadh Air's pilot recruitment has, until recently, meant one thing: the Boeing 787-9. That changed around late August 2026, when the airline began reaching out to candidates about a new Airbus A321neo track — both Direct Entry Captain and First Officer roles.

If you've been tracking Riyadh Air as a 787-only opportunity, it's time to update that picture.

What's New: The A321neo Track

Riyadh Air placed a large order for Airbus A321neo family aircraft to run alongside its Boeing 787-9 and Airbus A350-1000 fleets — a genuine three-type operation from a standing start. Since late August 2026, candidates already in Riyadh Air's applicant pool have started receiving outreach specifically about the A321neo programme, separate from the existing 787 pipeline.

Key numbers Three-type fleet strategy: Boeing 787-9, Airbus A350-1000, Airbus A321neo. The A321neo track opened for Direct Entry Captains and First Officers around 26 August 2026.

What's Confirmed

A few things about the A321neo track are well-established, independent of any single candidate's account:

  • Riyadh Air operates a three-type fleet strategy: Boeing 787-9 for long-haul, Airbus A350-1000 as an ultra-long-haul flagship, and Airbus A321neo/LR for medium-haul routes.
  • The panel interview process for A321 candidates uses the same 8-case question bank and format as the existing 787 process — a September 2026 A321 candidate confirmed this directly.
  • Riyadh Air operates its own full-motion A321neo simulators (alongside its 787 sims) at its training facility in Riyadh, operated by CAE.

What's Being Reported (Unconfirmed)

Not officially confirmed Everything in this section comes from candidates sharing their own experiences in community channels — not from an official Riyadh Air announcement. Treat it as a starting point for your own research, not a guarantee.

Candidates report that the A321neo rollout has come in phases: the earliest hires were Airbus Cadre/Training Captains and TREs, along with Saudi national candidates, before broadening to a wider pool of nationalities and experience profiles from around 20 September 2026 onward.

One candidate's Direct Entry Captain screening reportedly listed a requirement of around 1,500 hours PIC on Airbus and 5,000 hours total time — but this hasn't been confirmed through an official channel, and other candidates in the same discussion questioned whether it's a hard cutoff or a preference.

As of late September 2026, no candidate has publicly reported reaching the A321neo simulator stage — the track appears to still be working through its interview stages for most applicants.

How to Prepare

The good news for A321 candidates: because the panel interview format is shared with the 787 process, the preparation that already works for Riyadh Air's CBI and technical interview applies directly — CRM-driven decision making, risk mitigation and prevention, and high-workload management are the competency areas interviewers are listening for, regardless of which fleet you're applying for.
