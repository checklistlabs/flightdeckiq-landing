Phase 1 — Psychometric Profiling

Know Your Profile Before They Do

The WAVE personality assessment used by Gulf airlines measures 12 traits across 4 clusters. Most candidates walk in blind — unsure how they'll be perceived, and unable to contextualise their results. Understanding your profile, and knowing how to present it authentically, is one of the most underused edges in airline selection.

  • Full normative and ipsative assessment format — the same structure used in real selection
  • Detailed trait results with motive vs talent breakdown
  • Recruiter insight for every trait — what low scores signal to assessors and how to address them
  • Interview coaching questions directly linked to your profile
  • Abstract reasoning training with progressive difficulty
  • Video Interview Simulator with real AI feedback on every answer
Phase 2 — The HR & Technical Interview

Practise Until It Feels Effortless

Gulf airlines increasingly use recorded video interviews as an early screening stage. Most candidates underestimate it. FlightDeckIQ replicates the full experience — timed preparation, recording, and playback — with AI grading on every single answer using ICAO English Language Proficiency standards.

  • Real airline-style HR and competency-based interview questions

Phase 3 — Aptitude Testing & Simulator

Master the Tests Before You Walk In

The Emirates COMPASS test is the single biggest filter in their selection process. Candidates with thousands of hours fail it. It has nothing to do with how well you fly — it's about pattern recognition, abstract thinking, working memory, and performance under pressure. We train you for all of it.

  • Mathematical reasoning and ATPL technical question banks — fully tracked and scored
  • Spatial orientation and pattern recognition modules
  • Working memory test training across multiple formats
  • Multitasking exercises replicating dual-task assessment scenarios
  • Manual handling training for psychomotor assessment preparation
  • Detailed simulator profiles — handling tips and real insight into what the examiner is looking for
