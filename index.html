Master the Tests Before You Walk In

Phase 1 — Psychometric Profiling

Know Your Profile Before They Do

The WAVE personality assessment used by Gulf airlines measures 12 traits across 4 clusters. Understanding your profile — and how to present it — is one of the most underused edges in airline selection.

  • Full normative + ipsative assessment format (same as real selection)
  • Detailed trait results with motive vs talent breakdown
  • Recruiter insight for every trait — what low scores signal to assessors
  • Interview coaching questions linked to your profile
Phase 1 — COMPASS & Aptitude

The Test Most Pilots Don't Prepare For

The Emirates COMPASS test is the single biggest filter in their selection process. Candidates with thousands of hours fail it. It's not about flying — it's about pattern recognition, abstract thinking, and speed under pressure.

  • Abstract reasoning training with progressive difficulty
  • Spatial orientation and pattern recognition modules
  • Memory test training for working memory assessment
  • Multitasking exercises replicating dual-task scenarios
